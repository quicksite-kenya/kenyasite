Frequently Asked Questions

Consultation & Project FAQ

Everything you need to know before initiating your express website build in Kenya.

How does the 48-hour delivery guarantee work?

Once you submit your project brief and confirm your initial package setup, our Westlands engineering team immediately deploys your core framework. In 48 hours, your website will be live on a custom domain, complete with mobile optimization and M-Pesa integration ready for customers.

Can clients pay directly on my website via M-Pesa?

Yes! We connect your website to M-Pesa Paybill / Till API. When a customer enters their mobile number, an STK push pop-up appears on their phone requesting their M-Pesa PIN. Once confirmed, funds go straight into your business account.

How do I use the SAVE6 discount coupon code?

The discount code SAVE6 gives you 6% off any package setup fee or custom web service quote. Simply keep `SAVE6` in the intake form or mention it during your WhatsApp consultation with Michael Mulili.

Do you handle custom domain names and professional email addresses?

Yes, all subscription plans include custom domain configuration (e.g. `yourbusiness.co.ke`) and custom corporate email accounts (e.g. `info@yourbusiness.co.ke`).
