The Business Owner's Tech Guide

We believe in high transparency. Here are quick, simple answers to technical terms you see across web development and ads.

🔒 What is SSL + Secure Hosting?

Think of SSL as a digital seal of safety. Without it, modern web browsers show a scary "Not Secure" red warning to your visitors. We handle this completely so your clients feel 100% safe browsing and sending messages to your store.

📍 Why do I need Google Maps + SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) and Google Maps setup make sure that when someone in your area searches "best salon in Nairobi" or "trusted repair nearby," your business is the very first thing that pops up. It's like having a billboard on the busiest street corner.

📊 What is Meta Pixel or Ad Tracking?

It is a smart automatic visitor logbook. When we run Facebook/Instagram ads, the pixel tells the ad system exactly who visited your site and made a call. This is crucial because it keeps the ad algorithm smart, ensuring your daily budget is only spent on high-converting buyers.

📋 What is CRM & Lead Database?

Instead of writing customer details in paper files or losing track of WhatsApp messages, a CRM (Client Relationship Manager) saves every single person who fills out your form into a neat, secure online customer list. You can view all contacts, call them back, or add notes in one simple click!

💬 How does "Click-to-WhatsApp" work?

Instead of sending visitors to a boring form they don't want to fill out, these ads have a button that directly opens a WhatsApp chat with you on their phone. It pre-fills an introduction message, allowing the customer to start chatting with you with a single tap.

📅 Why do I need a Booking Calendar?

It acts like an automatic booking secretary. Clients visit your site, select an open time slot from your real-time calendar, and lock in their appointment. The system automatically updates your calendar and sends out friendly reminders so clients don't forget to show up.
